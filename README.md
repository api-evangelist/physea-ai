Physea Labs builds Liminal, a shared intelligence workspace that surfaces AI reasoning and sources, along with Liminal MCP for tool integration, Synth for synthetic customer testing, and custom AI models. The company offers a free tier with 50 solves and enterprise pricing, focusing on transparent, explainable AI for businesses and developers.

**Areas:** AI · MachineLearning · ExplainableAI · SaaS · Platform · Company

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Physea Labs: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
